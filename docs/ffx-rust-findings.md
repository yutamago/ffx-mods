## Not ported
- SPU2 model (ADSR stepping, interpolation kernel, reverb resampler),
  IPU block-decode details: hardware behaviour from PCSX2/PS1
  documentation, not FFX.exe facts.
- Encoding, gain, limiter, atlas packing, CLUT/name matching heuristics,
  bone reduction, the phase-2 music engine plan, `render.texture_filter`:
  demaster design, not findings.
- PS2 ELF/IRX addresses: ffx.sym holds FFX.exe only (IRX offsets are
  cited in ffx-rust's `ps2-music.md`, ELF `0x0018aba8` in `field.md`).
- Map VIF UV unpack, character UV fixed point,
  NPC mouths, battle quotes (row 17): not decoded or still open.
- `docs/font.md` says the text colours come from `0x00b5f890`; that is
  `TOGetTextureName`'s size table (ffx-rust `system.md`), not colours.
  Fix that doc.
- The alpha finding (`0x008423c0`/`0x008438b0`, ALPHA_2 from
  texture-list +8/+9) was ported from the unmerged branch
  `worktree-agent-a360f60ee4f087560`'s `docs/models.md`, checked against
  the dump.
